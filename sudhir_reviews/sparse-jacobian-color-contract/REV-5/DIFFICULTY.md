# DIFFICULTY — sparse-jacobian-color-contract REV-5

This task is medium, not easy, because a green single-batch rebuild is not enough. Packed-matrix correctness is a conjunction across several cooperating authorities at once: conflict-valid probe grouping with the exact conflict-required group count (not one group per unknown), resume seed stitching that must not leak prior bits into later groups, per-batch Euclidean residual magnitude with a step derived from that same batch under exact float arithmetic (no invented quantization), pack-preserving unpack orientation, and a residual operator whose first-order response includes a native row tint that is invisible in the bare term table or the Fortran assemble kernel alone. Frontier agents often repair most of those authorities and still collapse under all-or-nothing scoring when one last-mile contract fails—for example inventing a probe direction vector instead of the bundled host directions used for directional products, or “improving” the step formula with power-of-two rounding. High per-test pass rates therefore overstate ease: an 11/13 near-miss is reward 0.0, while NOP stays at 0% and oracle at 100%. The real work is holding the multi-authority end-to-end invariant across permutes, resumes, mixed-scale sequences, scale-equivalent tags, directional products, and ledger.groups—not a single sticky-gauge patch.
