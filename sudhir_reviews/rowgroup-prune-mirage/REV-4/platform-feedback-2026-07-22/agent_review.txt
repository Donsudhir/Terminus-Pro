SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This task presents a dual-language (Rust + C++) columnar storage system with
intentional bugs in three source files: the Rust marker computation ignores
liveness, the C++ slab-pruning planner omits legacy-generation handling, and the
C++ lattice reader ignores origin lanes. The agent must diagnose disagreements
between row-path and batch-path execution on archived mixed-generation stores,
fix the three buggy modules, and ensure fresh ingestion writes faithful markers
—all while preserving healthy-case behavior. The test suite contains 15 pytest
functions covering ingestion markers, selective/batch reads, absence probes,
cross-store consistency, idempotency, and text-code predicates.
